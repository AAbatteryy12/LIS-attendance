Enter the student's information, then place the RFID/NFC card near the reader and press Scan Card. On supported Android browsers, Web NFC can read/write NDEF-compatible NFC tags. If your reader exposes itself as a keyboard/USB device, use the RFID UID field directly.

Important: many NFC readers cannot be controlled directly by a normal HTML page. This demo uses Web NFC where supported. A USB/serial reader may require a native Android app, Web Serial, or a reader that acts like a keyboard.
